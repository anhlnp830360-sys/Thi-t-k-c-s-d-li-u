\section{USE CASE SPECIFICATIONS}\label{s:use_case_specifications}

Detailed description of how the main functions of AR-IMMS are executed, including actors, preconditions, main flow, alternative flows, and postconditions.

% UC-01
\subsection*{UC-01 -- Login}
\noindent
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|>{\bfseries}p{3.2cm}|X|}
\hline
Use Case ID & UC-01 \\ \hline
Use Case Name & Login \\ \hline
Primary Actor & Administrator / Operator / Technician \\ \hline
Preconditions & The user has a valid account. \\ \hline
Main Flow & 1. The user enters username and password. \newline
2. The system checks the login information. \newline
3. The system authenticates the account and role. \newline
4. The system grants access to functions corresponding to the permissions. \\ \hline
Alternative Flow & Invalid login information $\rightarrow$ the system displays an error message and requests a re-login. \\ \hline
Postconditions & The user logs in successfully and is granted permissions based on their role. \\ \hline
\end{tabularx}

% UC-02
\subsection*{UC-02 -- User Management}
\noindent
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|>{\bfseries}p{3.2cm}|X|}
\hline
Use Case ID & UC-02 \\ \hline
Use Case Name & User Management \\ \hline
Primary Actor & Administrator \\ \hline
Preconditions & The Administrator has logged in and has user management privileges. \\ \hline
Main Flow & 1. The Administrator opens User Management. \newline
2. The system displays the user list. \newline
3. The Administrator creates, updates, disables accounts, or changes roles. \newline
4. The system validates the data. \newline
5. The system saves the changes. \\ \hline
Alternative Flow & Invalid data or the account already exists $\rightarrow$ the system displays an error message and does not save the changes. \\ \hline
Postconditions & Account information and user permissions are updated. \\ \hline
\end{tabularx}

\hline
Use Case ID & UC-03 \\ \hline
Use Case Name & Infrastructure Management \\ \hline

\subsection*{UC-04 -- Monitor Real-time Telemetry}
\noindent
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|>{\bfseries}p{3.2cm}|X|}
\hline
Use Case ID & UC-04 \\ \hline
Use Case Name & Monitor Real-time Telemetry \\ \hline
Primary Actor & Operator \\ \hline
Preconditions & The Server/Node is registered, and the Collector is sending telemetry. \\ \hline
Main Flow & 1. The Operator selects the Server/Node to monitor. \newline
2. The Collector gathers operational parameters. \newline
3. The Backend receives and processes telemetry. \newline
4. The system displays CPU, RAM, Disk, Network, and status. \newline
5. Data is updated according to the configured cycle. \\ \hline
Alternative Flow & Telemetry is not received within the specified time $\rightarrow$ the system marks the device as unavailable and notifies the status. \\ \hline
Postconditions & The Operator can view the current status and telemetry of the device. \\ \hline
\end{tabularx}

% UC-05
\subsection*{UC-05 -- Manage Alert}
\noindent
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|>{\bfseries}p{3.2cm}|X|}
\hline
Use Case ID & UC-05 \\ \hline
Use Case Name & Manage Alert \\ \hline
Primary Actor & Monitoring System / Operator \\ \hline
Preconditions & The device is being monitored, and the threshold is configured. \\ \hline
Main Flow & 1. The Monitoring System receives telemetry. \newline
2. The system checks the data against the threshold. \newline
3. If the value exceeds the threshold, the system creates an Alert. \newline
4. The Operator views the Alert. \newline
5. The Operator acknowledges or updates the Alert status. \\ \hline
Alternative Flow & The value does not exceed the threshold $\rightarrow$ no Alert is created. A corresponding Alert already exists $\rightarrow$ the system updates the Alert instead of creating a duplicate record. \\ \hline
Postconditions & The Alert is recorded with the appropriate status and can be linked to the incident management process. \\ \hline
\end{tabularx}

% UC-06
\subsection*{UC-06 -- Incident Management}
\noindent
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|>{\bfseries}p{3.2cm}|X|}
\hline
Use Case ID & UC-06 \\ \hline
Use Case Name & Incident Management \\ \hline
Primary Actor & Operator \\ \hline
Preconditions & The Operator has logged in and there is an Alert or issue to investigate. \\ \hline
Main Flow & 1. The Operator selects the Alert/issue to be handled. \newline
2. Creates an Incident. \newline
3. Records incident details and the impact level. \newline
4. Links the Incident with the related device and Alert. \newline
5. Saves the Incident. \\ \hline
Alternative Flow & Incident information is incomplete $\rightarrow$ the system requests additional details before saving. \\ \hline
Postconditions & The Incident is created and ready for the Ticket creation/assignment step. \\ \hline
\end{tabularx}

% UC-07
\subsection*{UC-07 -- Ticket Management}
\noindent
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|>{\bfseries}p{3.2cm}|X|}
\hline
Use Case ID & UC-07 \\ \hline
Use Case Name & Ticket Management \\ \hline
Primary Actor & Operator \\ \hline
Preconditions & An Incident exists, or there is a maintenance request to be handled. \\ \hline
Main Flow & 1. The Operator creates a Maintenance Ticket from the Incident/Alert. \newline
2. Assigns the Ticket to a Technician. \newline
3. The Technician receives the Ticket. \newline
4. Involved parties update the Ticket status during the handling process. \newline
5. The Operator monitors and approves closing the Ticket. \\ \hline
Alternative Flow & The Ticket lacks information or processing is incomplete $\rightarrow$ cannot transition to the closed status. \\ \hline
Postconditions & The Ticket is updated to the correct status and the handling history is saved. \\ \hline
\end{tabularx}

% UC-08
\subsection*{UC-08 -- Maintenance}
\noindent
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|>{\bfseries}p{3.2cm}|X|}
\hline
Use Case ID & UC-08 \\ \hline
Use Case Name & Maintenance \\ \hline
Primary Actor & Technician \\ \hline
Preconditions & The Technician is assigned a maintenance Ticket. \\ \hline
Main Flow & 1. The Technician opens the Ticket. \newline
2. Identifies the device to be checked. \newline
3. Checks the status and operational information of the device. \newline
4. Records the root cause of the incident. \newline
5. Records the corrective action and handling results. \newline
6. Submits a request to complete/close the Ticket. \\ \hline
Alternative Flow & Cannot handle immediately $\rightarrow$ the Technician updates notes and status to resume handling later. \\ \hline
Postconditions & Maintenance results are saved, and the Ticket awaits the verification/closing step according to the process. \\ \hline
\end{tabularx}

% UC-10
\subsection*{UC-10 -- AR Device Identification and Information}
\noindent
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{|>{\bfseries}p{3.2cm}|X|}
\hline
Use Case ID & UC-10 \\ \hline
Use Case Name & AR Device Identification and Information \\ \hline
Primary Actor & Technician \\ \hline
Preconditions & The Technician has logged in; a device with a QR/ArUco Marker is registered and linked to the Server/Node. \\ \hline
Main Flow & 1. The Technician opens the AR application. \newline
2. The camera scans the QR/ArUco Marker on the device. \newline
3. The system recognizes the Marker ID. \newline
4. The system maps the Marker ID to the corresponding Server/Node. \newline
5. The system retrieves information from the Backend. \newline
6. AR displays information such as the device, status, telemetry, Alert, and Workload at the physical location. \\ \hline
Alternative Flow & Cannot recognize the Marker or the Marker is unregistered $\rightarrow$ the system displays an error message. Cannot retrieve data $\rightarrow$ displays disconnected/no data status. \\ \hline
Postconditions & The Technician correctly identifies the device and has direct operational information in the AR environment. \\ \hline
\end{tabularx}
